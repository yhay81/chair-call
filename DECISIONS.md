# Product decisions

## Public calls without applicant storage

The first test needs real listings and contact starts, not an internal chat system. Chair Call stores a short public call and sends applicants to an allowlisted social contact route after composing a message locally.

## No photos or exact addresses

Face and salon photos, phone numbers, email addresses, exact addresses, and applicant submissions increase privacy and moderation exposure before they prove the routing wedge. The first release accepts only a salon display name, broad Tokyo area, nearest station, service conditions, times, and an allowlisted HTTPS contact URL.

## Visual workbench

The interface is organized as a salon chair, tool tray, schedule rail, and compact call cards. Headings stay small and no oversized marketing hero or experiment copy appears in the product.

## Short retention

Calls expire within 14 days and are deleted 30 days after expiry. Anonymous events expire after 35 days. Publishers can close calls immediately with a fragment-held management token.
