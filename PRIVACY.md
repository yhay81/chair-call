# Privacy

- Public listing fields are stored in D1 until 30 days after their 14-day expiry.
- Exact addresses, phone numbers, faces, applicant profiles, applications, payment, and chat are not accepted.
- Management tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes.
- Anonymous event identifiers are hashed and event rows are deleted after 35 days.
- Application messages and selected appointment slots are assembled only in the applicant's browser.
